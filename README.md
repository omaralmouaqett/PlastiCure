## Ideonella sakaiensis / PETase

Yoshida et al., "A bacterium that degrades and assimilates poly(ethylene terephthalate)," Science (2016).

https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad6359

Han et al., "Structural insight into molecular mechanism of poly(ethylene terephthalate) degradation," Nature Communications (2018).

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-02881-1

The page describes PETase converting PET to MHET and MHETase hydrolysing MHET into terephthalic acid and ethylene glycol.
